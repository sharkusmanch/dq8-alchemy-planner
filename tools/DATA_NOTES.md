# Data notes: items and recipes

Generated by `python3 tools/build_items_recipes.py` from the reference sources (not committed).
Rerunning the script rewrites `src/data/items.json` and `src/data/recipes.json`.

## Items

- 356 items: the 387-entry table in the RA code note at `0x409a10` (game ids 0-386), minus
  `Nothing` and 30 `DUMMY` entries. Highest game id is 386 (Ice Shield).
- 322 item cards in the Red_Scarlet guide carry a `Buy price` line; each maps to exactly one item.
  The remaining 34 items (key items, orbs, memos, recipe books) have category `other` and no
  prices. The one card under "Important Items" is also `other`.
- Category comes from the list header that encloses the card. Cheeses have no header of their
  own: a card in the Items List whose title ends in "Cheese" is `cheese` (15 items).
- Only gold prices are kept. Casino-token prices (12 cards) give no `buy`; "Not buyable" gives no
  `buy`; "Not sellable" gives no `sell`.
- One card (Orichalcum) has its top border fused onto the title line; the parser accepts that.
- `tools/curated/price_overrides.json` is applied last. It holds one entry, Saint's Ashes; see
  "Price disagreements" below.

## Name aliases

`tools/aliases.json` maps source spellings to canonical item names. Matching is otherwise an
exact, case-insensitive comparison with the canonical name; anything else stops the build.

- Checklist: Battle axe, Conquerer's Axe, Garter belt, Slime earring, Highly strung cheese.
- Code-note recipe labels: Thief key, Sknakeskin whip, Sorceror's ring, Spiker armour,
  Tempar's uniform, Mercury rapier.
- Code-note ingredient hint: Boxers (Boxer Shorts).
- Red_Scarlet card title: Conquerer's Axe.
- Dragon's Den spellings: Amor Seco Essense, Rose Wort, Rose Root, Prayer's Ring, Way Farer's Clothes.

## Recipe numbering

- Checklist: 184 recipes (rows 2-185 of sheet 2; the last row, Chain mail, is the one the
  walkthrough misses). 120 have two ingredients and 64 have three.
- Code notes `0x40e87d`-`0x40e934` give the 184 recipe numbers. Each note is matched to a
  checklist recipe by result name. Eleven results have two recipes; the note's bracketed
  ingredient hint settles all of them except Greater panacea (recipe 3 has the hint, recipe 4 is
  the remainder).
- Evidence for numbering: the real save has exactly eleven completed recipes, at numbers
  0, 1, 6, 9, 11, 15, 62, 71, 93, 141, 170. They resolve to Strong medicine, Special medicine,
  Rose-root, Strong antidote, Special antidote, Thief's key, Iron lance, Iron axe, Wayfarer's
  clothes, Feathered cap and Mild cheese, as the brief expected. Recipe 6 is the two-ingredient
  Rose-root (Medicinal herb + Strong medicine); recipe 5 is the three-herb one. No pin needed
  correcting.
- A one-off comparison against the Red_Scarlet Alchemy Pot Mixes List matched 181 of its
  181 parseable lines to generated recipes; three recipes (Zombie mail from Silver mail +
  Zombiesbane, Chain mail, Mercury's rapier) were not picked up by that parse.

## Unconfirmed: recipes 16 and 22 (Elfin elixir / Magic water)

- Two notes (`0x40e88d` = recipe 16, `0x40e893` = recipe 22) are both labelled "Magic water"; no
  note says "Elfin elixir". The checklist has one Magic water (Holy water + Seed of magic) and
  one Elfin elixir (Magic water + Yggdrasil dew). One of the two notes is mislabelled.
- The data says recipe 16 = Elfin elixir and recipe 22 = Magic water. **This is not confirmed in
  the game.** The sources do not decide it; it rests on three indications from published saves
  and the item table, all pointing the same way:
  1. One played save has recipe 16 completed and recipe 22 untouched. It holds 87 Magic Waters
     in the bag (a quantity that is bought, not brewed), no Seed of Magic, 8 Elfin Elixirs on
     the characters, and has Yggdrasil dew (recipe 18) completed. That is what a player who
     brewed Elfin elixir and never brewed Magic water looks like.
  2. With Magic water at 22, recipes 18-22 make items 275, 276, 277, 278, 279, an unbroken run.
     Elfin elixir (280) at 22 would skip one.
  3. In three saves from after the ending, recipe 16 is learned but not made, and in no late
     save from before the ending is it learned. The game teaches recipe 16 late, which suits
     Elfin elixir, and it sits between two other recipes that are taught by a hint (Thief's key
     at 15, Timbrel of tension at 17).
- Until the previous revision the data had the two the other way round, as a plain guess (the
  lower number to Magic water). The owner's early save has both status bytes at zero and says
  nothing either way.
- To confirm: brew Magic water (Holy water + Seed of magic) in the game and look at the save.
  Exactly one of the status bytes for recipes 16 and 22 (file offsets `0x5A8D` and `0x5A93`) gets
  bit 0 set. If it is the byte of recipe 16, swap the two results back in `build_recipes`.
- Until then a wrong assignment shows up as the wrong one of the two recipes ticked after a save
  import. The Recipes tab marks both with "save number unconfirmed", the manual done-toggle on
  each corrects it, and the two recipes are otherwise unaffected (their ingredients and results
  are certain; only their numbers may be swapped).

# Data notes: stages, locations and shops

Generated by `python3 tools/build_shops.py`. `tools/curated/stages.json` and
`tools/curated/locations.json` are written by hand and copied to `src/data/`; `shops.json` is parsed
from the Red_Scarlet "Town Shops List". `--strict` makes the build fail while a price disagreement
is unsettled; without it the disagreements are printed and the files are still written.

## Stages

A stage is the point in the story at which a set of places first becomes reachable. Order follows
the two chronological walkthroughs (Red_Scarlet part 1 and Dunatis). **A place sits at the stage at
which it can first be entered, not the one at which a walkthrough gets round to it.** The save
import depends on this: it takes the player's stage from the latest-staged Zoom destination that
is unlocked (and from the pot and the story bytes, below), so a town staged later than it can be
reached would push an imported save forward.

Findings that differ from the task brief:

- **The basic pot is given on the ferry, not on leaving Peregrin Quay.** Both walkthroughs have
  Trode hand it over in the hold during the crossing from Port Prospect, and both use it as soon
  as the party lands (the thief's key is mixed in Peregrin Quay). So `basic-pot` is the stage that
  opens Peregrin Quay; there is no moment in Peregrin Quay without the pot.
- **The improved pot** is confirmed for the point after Medea is recovered. The wagon (and the pot
  with it) is stolen on entering Pickham and returned after the Swordsman's Labyrinth. Trode then
  upgrades the pot over the next two or so inn stays; both walkthroughs see it at the Hilltop Hut.
  `pickham` is therefore the last stage with the basic pot, and the pot is missing for the part of
  it between the theft and the return.
- **The instant pot** is a reward chosen after beating a Dragovian trial, so `postgame` is the
  trials. The Dragovian Sanctuary and its shop are reachable earlier in the post-ending game, while
  the improved pot is still in use; they have their own stage, `dragovian-sanctuary`.

### Stages a save proves without a Zoom destination

Five stages have no Zoom destination: `argonia-bazaar`, `dragon-graveyard`, `purgatory-island`,
`black-citadel` and `postgame`. A save import takes the latest of three things: the stage of
its latest Zoom destination, the stage at which its pot arrives (`goals.potStages`), and any
stage whose `storyMin` or `trialMin` in `tools/curated/stages.json` the save has reached.

- `storyMin` is compared with the main story chapter, one byte at `0x4092b0` (file offset
  `0x4B0`). Its code note lists the chapters and what starts each. JSON has no hexadecimal, so
  the file holds 11 and 17 for `0x0b` and `0x11`.
- `trialMin` is compared with the number of Dragovian trials beaten, one byte at `0x408fe6`
  (file offset `0x1E6`, values 0 to 6).
- `build_shops.py` checks the values are whole numbers from 1 to 255 and that `storyMin` never
  falls from one stage to a later one.

| Stage | Proved by | Why that value |
|---|---|---|
| `argonia-bazaar` | story chapter >= `0x0b` | Chapter `0x0b` starts when the magic mirror is claimed in Argonia. The king hands it over only after the hunt and the bazaar scene, so the stalls are open by then. The note describes no chapter between `0x07` (the ship) and `0x0b`, so no earlier value is certain. No save examined sits at this chapter; the value rests on the note |
| `black-citadel` | story chapter >= `0x11` | Chapter `0x11` starts when Neos is destroyed, which is the stage's own definition. Seen in two published saves that had imported as `flight` |
| `postgame` | trials beaten >= 1 | The first trial beaten is the stage's definition. Seen in six published saves |
| `dragon-graveyard` | nothing | The Dragon Graveyard is an optional side quest; no chapter of the main story says it is done. A save that has finished it but not yet reached Empycchu imports one stage early, as `orkutsk` |
| `purgatory-island` | nothing | The escape from Purgatory Island falls inside chapter `0x10`, which starts earlier (at Tryan Gully), and the next chapter is already Neos destroyed. A save between the escape and Neos imports one stage early, as `flight` |

The last two are left to the player's stage selector on purpose: a stage that shows one step
early hides a shop for a while, a stage that shows late would show places that are not open.
Other notes do record both events (the Casino Heirs scenario byte at `0x4092ce` reaches `0x0b`
when the casino reopens, and the byte at `0x408fc8` is 2 after the escape); they are not read.
The owner's early save has chapter `0x03` and stays at `maella-abbey`, its Zoom stage.

### Places the ship reaches (moved to `ship` in Task 5)

Earlier these sat at `baccarat` or `argonia`, following the order of the Dunatis walkthrough. Each
was checked against both walkthroughs for a story event that has to happen first; none was found.

| Place | Was | Evidence that the ship is enough |
|---|---|---|
| Savella Cathedral | `baccarat` | Red_Scarlet visits and prints its three shops in the ship chapter (II-G6), before the western continent. Dunatis: nothing to do there but two treasures and the shops |
| Desert Chapel | `argonia` | Red_Scarlet II-G5, same chapter, item shop printed; reached on foot from a landing on the Argonia continent |
| Seaview Church | `baccarat` | First stop on the western continent in both. Red_Scarlet tells a player who finds the sea tour too hard to go there straight away; Dunatis goes from Neos |
| Baccarat (town) | `baccarat` | Both walk in from Seaview Church. Dunatis buys Poison Needles and a Coral Hairpin on that first visit; Red_Scarlet prints both shops on arrival and says only the casino is closed. The casino keeps its own location and stage |
| Argonia (town) | `argonia` | Red_Scarlet lands and enters in the ship chapter (II-G2): the party can collect items and buy equipment although the story has not sent it there. The castle is a different matter, below |
| Campsite west of Argonia | `argonia` | Not one of the five towns the review named; moved on the same rule because it has a shop. Dunatis buys a Turtle Shell there on the tour of the region before the castle opens; Red_Scarlet lists its stock in II-H3, before its Argonia chapter |

Left at their later stage, with the reason:

- **Argonia castle** (new location `argonia-castle`, stage `argonia`). Both walkthroughs say the
  castle cannot be entered at first: the party has to hear of Golding's death in Baccarat, go to
  the Dark Ruins barrier and talk to the man waiting there. The cards' "Argonia 1F/3F/4F" entries are
  castle floors, so `build_sources.py` places them in the castle; otherwise moving the town to
  `ship` would have made eight castle sources look collectable two stages early.
- **Royal Hunting Ground** (`argonia`): entered only with Prince Charmles, on the king's errand.
- **Baccarat casino** (`dragon-graveyard`): shut until the Dragon Graveyard quest is done.
- **Argonia bazaar stalls** (`availableFrom: argonia-bazaar`): open after the hunt.
- **Chateau Felix** and the **Dark Ruins** island (`baccarat`): no event gates them (Chateau Felix
  asks three questions at the door), so by the rule they could sit at `ship` too. They were left
  because they have no shop and only three sources between them, and because moving them would
  leave `baccarat` empty. Staging them late hides those sources for one stage; it invents nothing.
- **Mystical Spring** (`argonia`): no shop and no source. Dunatis walks past it before the castle
  opens; whether its Zoom flag can be set that early is not confirmed.

The stages `baccarat` and `argonia` are kept. `argonia` gates the castle and the hunting ground.
`baccarat` gates nothing; it is kept as a story point a player recognises, and because both
walkthroughs do Chateau Felix and the Dark Ruins barrier there. Dropping it would be a one-line
change in `tools/curated/stages.json` plus moving its two places to `ship`.

Where the walkthroughs differ, or the choice is a judgement:

- **Port Prospect** can be walked to from Alexandria before the Tower of Alexandra (Dunatis
  suggests it as an option). Both guides do the tower first, so it has its own later stage. By the
  rule above it belongs at `alexandria`; it was not part of the Task 5 review and is unchanged.
- **Pirate's Cove** is entered early by both guides (three items, Zoom flag) but can only be
  finished at the story point; it sits at `ship` and has no shop.
- **Dark Ruins** sits at `baccarat` because the Zoom flag is set on the first visit to the barrier;
  the ruins themselves open with the Sun Mirror, after the bazaar.
- **Arcadia**: a checkpoint north of Argonia blocks the road until the Dark Ruins boss is beaten
  (Dunatis). Red_Scarlet also first goes there afterwards.
- **Dragon Graveyard** is an optional side quest, available once the Dark Ruins boss is beaten.
  Dunatis does it between the first Orkutsk visit and the Herb Grotto, Red_Scarlet after the Herb
  Grotto; both do it after reaching Orkutsk and before the Pirate's Cove, so the stage sits there.
  A player who skips the quest has no Baccarat casino whatever stage they pick.
- **Morrie's Place**, the **Riverside Chapel** and the **Swordsman's Labyrinth** are on the road of
  the stage they are listed under; no separate gate was found for them.

## Locations

- 55 locations: the 34 Zoom destinations, two shop sites without a Zoom entry (West Trodain Church,
  Campsite west of Argonia), the two casino prize counters and 17 places that exist only to give
  sources a stage (listed under "Placing sources"; Argonia castle is the newest).
- `zoomBit` = (address - `0x409090`) x 8 + bit, from the code notes at `0x409090`-`0x409094`. The
  script reads the notes and stops if any curated `zoomBit` differs. Note spellings mapped by hand:
  Pereguin Quay, Princess Minnie's, Chateaux Felix, Royal Hunting Grounds.
- Empycchu and Dark Empycchu share one heading and one stock list in the shops list; there is one
  location (`empycchu`, the Zoom destination) and one shop.
- Lakeside Cabin (inn only) and the Chapel of Autumn have no shop and no Zoom entry and are not
  locations.

- No new aliases were needed: every name in the list matches a canonical item name.

## Price disagreements

Every gold price in the shops list is compared with `buy` in `items.json`. There is one
disagreement, settled in `tools/curated/price_overrides.json`:

| Item | Shops list | Item card | Chosen | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `saints-ashes` | 300 at the Dragovian Sanctuary item shop | 12,000 (sell 6,000) | **12,000** | The Sanctuary chapter of the Red_Scarlet walkthrough (part 1) prints the shop with Saint's Ashes at 12,000 and says outright that the Japanese release charges 300 gold and the North American release 12,000. So the shops list carries the Japanese price. This app is for the USA disc. The card's sell price, 6,000, is half of 12,000, as sell prices are throughout. Dunatis prints no gold price for it, but buys all 17 it needs with casino tokens and only Devil's Tails at the Sanctuary shop, which fits 12,000 and not 300 |

The override repeats the card's value, so `items.json` did not change; its job is to mark the
disagreement as settled, which lets `python3 tools/build_shops.py --strict` pass. The shops list
has no other price that differs from a card.

Not confirmed from two sources: the stage of the second Neos item shop (one walkthrough mentions
when it appears), and that the campsite merchant stays for the rest of the game.

# Data notes: sources

Generated by `python3 tools/build_sources.py` (run `tools/build_shops.py` first after changing
`tools/curated/locations.json`). Reads the Red_Scarlet item cards, the Town Shops List's prize
counters and the Mini Medals prize list. No new aliases were needed.

- 824 sources for 165 of the 203 ingredients: 323 found, 27 gift, 6 medal, 10 casino, 241 drop,
  217 rare-drop. The other 38 ingredients are sold in a shop or produced by a recipe and their cards
  list no found, drop or prize entries; non-ingredient items get nothing.
- Cards wrap lists across lines and sometimes fuse two card columns with `||`; both are joined
  before splitting on commas outside brackets. Every drop names a monster that has its own card.
- Cross-checks the script asserts: the 12 casino token prices agree between the prize counters and
  the cards' `Buy price` lines (only the 10 ingredients are written); the 11 Mini Medal prizes
  agree with the counts on their cards (6 are ingredients).
- `where` labels are our own wording, at most eight words (the build stops at nine). Place names
  are kept as the card gives them, with the container (chest, barrel, pot, dresser, cupboard,
  bag), except five that read as directions rather than a name; `SHORT_PLACES` gives those a
  plain label ("Northwest of Riverside Chapel: chest", "South end of the Alexandria gate: chest",
  "Far east of Chateau Felix: chest", and two near Argonia). The location is still resolved from
  the card's wording. Prose entries are matched by explicit rules in `PROSE`; an entry that
  matches nothing stops the build.
- Starting equipment (Hero, Yangus, Jessica, Angelo) is a `gift` at Farebury.

## Places split out because an event gates them

A source takes its stage from its location, so an area that opens later than the town or dungeon
around it is its own location. Stages were checked against Red_Scarlet part 1 (RS) and Dunatis.

| Location | Stage | What it holds, and the evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `monster-arena` | `improved-pot` | Rank G, E and D prizes. RS F5 (after Trodain and the cleanup, level 22) fights these ranks |

These cannot be placed more precisely from the cards or the two guides:

  at its town's stage.
- Fresh Milk from cows is unplaced (below).

# Data notes: goals

`tools/curated/goals.json` is written by hand. `python3 tools/build_goals.py` checks it against
the other data files and the code notes and writes it to `src/data/goals.json`, turning the RAM
addresses of the save signs (below) into offsets into the save file. Build order after a change to the
curated files: `build_items_recipes.py`, `build_shops.py --strict`, `build_sources.py`,
`build_goals.py`.

## Pots and milestones

- `brewThresholds`: 30 brews in the basic pot, 100 in the improved pot (task brief).
- `potStages`: `basic-pot`, `improved-pot`, `postgame`; see "Stages" for what was found about each.
- `milestones`: 10, 25, 50, 100, 150 and 184 recipes, the six recipe-count achievements.

## Dodgy Dave

Six requests, in the order he asks. Three sources give the same order:

| # | He wants | First-time reward | Second time round |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Special Medicine | 500 gold | 400 gold |
| 2 | Reinforced Boomerang | 1,200 gold | 1,000 gold |
| 3 | Ring of Immunity | Bandit Axe | 3,500 gold |
| 4 | Robe of Serenity | 6,000 gold | 4,000 gold |
| 5 | Sandstorm Spear | Happy Hat | 6,000 gold |
| 6 | Crimson Robe | Big Boss Shield | 12,000 gold |

- Order: the code note at `0x40eac0` numbers the position in his sequence 0-5 in exactly this
  order and says it returns to 0 after the Crimson Robe. The per-item counters at
  `0x40ea80`-`0x40ea8a` are laid out in the same order. The Dunatis list and the Red_Scarlet list
  (part 1, II-E5) agree, and the Red_Scarlet item cards call the Bandit Axe, Happy Hat and Big
  Boss Shield his 3rd, 5th and 6th items.
- The first reward is 500 gold: the code note at `0x40ea80` and Red_Scarlet both say 500 the
  first time and 400 on later rounds. Dunatis prints 400 for it, which is the later-round figure.
- The second round pays gold only and is not a goal; `goals.dave` holds the first round. The RA
  guide's second Dave achievement is earned when the sequence returns to its start.
- Dave can move on without a hand-in: two published saves have his sequence ahead of his trade
  counters (requests never handed in lie behind the one he asks for). Red_Scarlet attributes it to
  declining a trade while holding the item; the saves do not show the cause. So the state keeps one
  flag per request plus the request he wants now, and the More tab can set either by hand.
- The Bandit Axe and the Happy Hat are themselves ingredients (Bandit mail, Elevating shoes).

## Scarce items

The rule: an ingredient is scarce when nothing can supply it again and again. That is, no shop
stocks it, no casino prize counter offers it, no recipe makes it, and no monster has it as its
normal drop. Rare drops do not count as a supply. Slime Crown is added by name, because the RA
guide singles it out and its normal drop, from the Metal King Slime at 1 in 64, is no real
supply. `build_goals.py` and `goals.test.ts` both stop if the list and the rule differ.

A boss that is fought once is not a monster that can be farmed. `build_sources.py` (`BOSS_DROPS`)
writes the guaranteed drop of such a boss as a `found` source at the place of the fight, and
fails the build if one of its rules stops matching:

| Boss | Drop | Placed at | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Geyzer | Seed of Strength | `waterfall-cave` | Type-data code note: boss, guaranteed drop. Monster card: one fight, Waterfall Cave level 3 |
| Tortured Soul | Gold Rosary | `ruined-abbey` | Code note: boss, guaranteed. Card: one fight, Ruined Abbey level 3 |
| Empyrea | Seed of Magic | `empycchu` | Code note: boss, guaranteed. Card: one fight, west of Dark Empycchu |
| Megalodon | Yggdrasil Dew | `black-citadel` | Code note: boss, guaranteed. Card: Black Citadel level 4, not a random encounter |
| Ruin | Orichalcum | `black-citadel` | Code note: boss, guaranteed. Card: Black Citadel. Both walkthroughs record the Orichalcum |

Red Horn, Blue Fang and the Servant of Darkness also carry a boss or event entry in the code
notes, but their cards list a second place where they are met as ordinary monsters, and their
drops are chances (1 in 16 to 1 in 256), so they stay `drop` and `rare-drop`. No other boss drops
an ingredient. Two consequences for the scarce list: Orichalcum is on it by the rule itself (its
only normal drop was the Ruin's), and Seed of Strength joins it (its only normal drop was
Geyzer's). Gold Rosary is sold, Yggdrasil Dew is brewed, and Seed of Magic is still dropped by
the Democrobot, so those three stay off.

Left out on purpose: **Falcon Blade, Liquid Metal Armour, Rune Staff and Silver Platter**. No shop

each for tokens without limit (10,000, 50,000, 3,000 and 500), and the data keeps prize counters
as `casino` sources, not shops. Dunatis buys all four there in bulk. Listing them would show a
permanent shortage warning for things tokens can always buy. They come back into scope only if a
player never opens the Baccarat casino (Falcon Blade and Liquid Metal Armour; the latter also has

Also not scarce by the rule, but worth knowing: the Seeds of Agility, Defence and Magic each have
a normal drop from a monster that can be fought again (Liquid Metal Slime, Metal Slime and
Democrobot). Each has 8 to 13 one-time sources and is listed by one or two recipes, so nothing
is at risk unless the seeds are eaten.

## Save signs for scarce sources

A save records which one-time sources are gone, so an import ticks them off. Each scarce source
in `tools/curated/goals.json` carries one sign, and all 54 have one:

- `flag`: a bit that the game sets when the chest, barrel or pot is emptied, or when the event
  that hands the item over has happened. 48 sources.
- `medalPrize`: the source is the nth prize Princess Minnie gives. The count of prizes received
  is a 16-bit value at `0x408fae`; its note lists the eleven prizes in order (the Gold Nugget is
  the 4th, the Miracle Sword the 6th, the Orichalcum the 8th, the Dangerous Bustier the 10th).
  Reading the value as a count is an inference from that note; the values seen in saves are 0,
  6, 10 and 11, and they fit. 4 sources.
- `killCount`: the 16-bit kill count of a boss that is fought once and always drops the item
  (Geyzer, the Ruin). In every save examined the boss's first-item drop count is at least 1
  exactly when the kill count is. The Ruin also has an event flag (`0x409541` bit 3), which
  agrees with its kill count in every save. 2 sources.

How each was checked:

1. **Against the code notes, by the build.** `build_goals.py` stops unless the note at a flag's
   address has a line for that bit that names the item, the nth line of the prize note is the
   item (and the label gives that many medals), and the note at a kill-count address is the kill
   count of the monster the label names. Four notes word the item differently and are listed in
   `NOTE_WORDING`: two misspellings, and the Trolls' Maze reward, which is a single event that
   hands over both kinds of seed (so `seed-of-life-13` and `seed-of-strength-12` share a flag).
2. **Against saves.** Fourteen distinct saves published by other players and the owner's early
   save were decoded. "Late saves" below counts the six played saves from after the ending in
   which the sign is set. No sign is set in the three files saved in the first minutes of the
   game. The last column names the early saves (story chapter 4 or lower) that already have it.
3. **Against the owner's bag.** The owner's save has eight signs set: three Seeds of Life, one
   Seed of Wisdom and four Seeds of Strength. Its bag holds exactly three, one and four.

## Scarce arithmetic: Orichalcum, Slime Crown, Gold Nugget

Computed by `build_goals.py` from `recipes.json` (it prints the table and stops if a figure
changes). Every recipe is brewed once. A recipe has to be brewed again when recipes consume more
of its product than the recipes making it supply, and nothing but the pot supplies that product.

| | Recipes that list it | Forced repeat brews | Graph figure | RA guide | One-time sources | To farm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orichalcum | 7 | +1 | **8** | 7 | 6 | 2 |
| Slime Crown | 3 | +1 | **4** | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Gold Nugget | 3 | +1 | **4** | not mentioned | 3 | 1 |

- Orichalcum is listed by Sage's stone (23), Meteorite bracer (26), Goddess ring (29), Liquid metal
  sword (53), Megaton hammer (78), Metal king armour (119) and Metal king shield (139).
- Slime Crown is listed by Liquid metal sword (53), King axe (72) and Metal king armour (119).
- Gold Nugget is listed by Sage's stone (23), Golden axe (75) and Golden tiara (159).
- **Liquid metal sword is brewed twice.** Two recipes consume one, Rusty old sword (54) and
  Dragovian king sword (55); one recipe makes it (53: Rusty old sword + Slime Crown + Orichalcum);
  no shop, chest, prize or monster supplies it. The second brew costs one more Orichalcum and one
  more Slime Crown. It does not need a second Rusty old sword from outside: recipe 54 turns the
  first Liquid metal sword back into one.
- **Golden axe is brewed twice.** King axe (72) and Moon axe (73) each consume one; recipe 75
  (Iron axe + Gold Nugget) is its only supply. That is one more Gold Nugget.
- Metal king shield is consumed once (Ruinous shield, 136) and made once, and Meteorite bracer is
  consumed once (Uber falcon blade, 46) and made once, so neither adds a brew.
- **The RA guide says 6 Orichalcum come naturally and 1 more is needed (7), and 2 Slime Crowns come
  naturally and 1 more is needed (3).** The six and the two match the one-time sources above. The
  seven and the three are the recipes that list each item, counted once; the guide does not count
  the second Liquid metal sword. Dunatis does: his list brews the Liquid metal sword recipe again
